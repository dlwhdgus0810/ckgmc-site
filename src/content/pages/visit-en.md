<h2 class="h4">Welcome to our church</h2>
<p>Whether you are new to the area or visiting church for the first time, we would love to meet you. Let the welcome desk know this is your first visit so we can help you find the right room.</p>
</section>

<section id="visit-parking" class="mb-5">
<h2>Directions, parking and entrance</h2>
<p>We are at <strong>9400 Nall Ave, Overland Park, KS 66207</strong>. You may park in front of or alongside the church. Enter through the <strong>main entrance on the west side</strong> of the building and stop at the welcome desk.</p>
<p><a class="btn btn-outline-primary" href="https://www.google.com/maps/dir/?api=1&amp;destination=9400%20Nall%20Ave%2C%20Overland%20Park%2C%20KS%2066207" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Open driving directions <span class="sr-only">(new tab)</span></a></p>
<p>If you need help with access or getting around the building, please contact the church before your visit.</p>
</section>

<section id="visit-children" class="mb-5">
<h2>Bringing children or teenagers?</h2>
<h3 class="h5">Toddlers and children</h3>
<p>Children's worship and Bible study meet on <strong>Sunday at 11:00 AM in the children's room</strong>. Tell the welcome desk your child's age and ask for directions when you arrive.</p>
<h3 class="h5">Youth</h3>
<p>Middle and high school students worship with the English Service. Ask us about youth gatherings and the current YES! Ministry schedule.</p>
<p><a href="/ministries/english">Meet our English Ministry</a> · <a href="#visit-contact">Ask about children or youth</a></p>
</section>

<section id="visit-next" class="mb-5">
<h2>What happens when I arrive?</h2>
<ol>
<li><strong>Say hello at the welcome desk.</strong> We can help you find the worship space or children's program.</li>
<li><strong>Join us for worship.</strong> After worship, you are welcome to share a meal and meet others in the fellowship hall.</li>
<li><strong>Stay connected.</strong> If you would like a church leader to follow up, use the newcomer registration form or contact the church. We can explain newcomer gatherings and the membership process.</li>
</ol>
